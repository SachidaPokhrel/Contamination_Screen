# Pseudomonas CIP-10 specificity review

Analysis used 100,000 paired-end fragments per sample.

Competitive mapping retained:
- DNA: 143 reads from 116 fragments; read fraction 0.0715%.
- RNA: 128 reads from 115 fragments; read fraction 0.0640%.

All retained DNA alignments overlapped annotated rRNA features on
CP087163.1. For RNA, 126 alignments overlapped those rRNA features,
one overlapped a chromosome tRNA-Gln feature, and one lay within sucD.

Both mates of the sucD-supporting pair had matches to Phytophthora
transcripts in an unrestricted NCBI nt search. Representative
rRNA-supporting pairs also matched oomycete and other eukaryotic
references, frequently with full-length, high-identity alignments.

Species-specific support for Pseudomonas sp. CIP-10 was not demonstrated.
The exact origins of the supporting reads remain unresolved.
Representative searches do not establish the origin of every read.
Database error and additional organisms were not established.

Mapping measurements are retained unchanged.
Significance bin and likely source remain for user assessment.
